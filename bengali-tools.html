Bengali tools

Bijoy to Unicode, বাংলা calendar, amounts in words and Bengali OCR.

  • Word counter: Count words, characters, sentences and reading time, including Bengali text.
  • Bengali digit converter: Convert numbers between English digits (0–9) and Bengali digits (০–৯).
  • OCR: image or PDF to text: Read the text in photos, screenshots and scanned PDFs, in English and বাংলা.
  • Bijoy to Unicode: Convert old Bijoy (SutonnyMJ) Bangla text or Word files to Unicode বাংলা.
  • Bengali calendar: Convert dates between English and বাংলা সন (Bangladesh calendar).
  • Amount in words: Write amounts in words for cheques and invoices, in English (lakh, crore) and বাংলা.
  • Unicode to Bijoy: Convert Unicode Bengali into Bijoy (SutonnyMJ) text for older software.
