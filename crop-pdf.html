Crop PDF

Trim the margins of PDF pages by the amounts you choose.

How to use it

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Enter the margins to remove, in millimetres.
  3. Download the cropped PDF.

Questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser. Files are read into memory on your device and are never sent to a server.

Related tools

  • Pages per sheet: Put 2, 4, 6, 8, 9 or 16 pages on each sheet to save paper when printing.
  • Rotate PDF: Turn all pages, or only the pages you choose, by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
