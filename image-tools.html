Image tools

Convert, compress, resize and clean up photos and graphics.

  • Images to PDF: Turn JPG, PNG and WebP images into a single PDF.
  • Convert image: Convert images between JPG, PNG and WebP, plus AVIF where your browser supports it.
  • JPG to PNG: Convert JPG photos to lossless PNG images.
  • PNG to JPG: Convert PNG images to smaller JPG files, with a background for transparent areas.
  • Compress image: Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP images and see exactly how much you saved.
  • Resize image: Change image dimensions by pixels or percentage, keeping the aspect ratio.
  • Rotate or flip image: Rotate images by 90° steps or mirror them horizontally or vertically.
  • Remove photo metadata: Strip EXIF, GPS location and camera details from photos without losing quality.
  • Image to Base64: Encode an image as a Base64 data URI for HTML, CSS or JSON.
  • QR code generator: Make QR codes for links, text or Wi-Fi, and download them as PNG or SVG.
  • OCR: image or PDF to text: Read the text in photos, screenshots and scanned PDFs, in English and বাংলা.
  • Crop image: Crop a photo freely or to a set shape like square, 16:9 or passport size.
  • Image filters: Adjust brightness, contrast and saturation, or make images grayscale, sepia or inverted.
  • Watermark image: Add a text watermark, in English or বাংলা, to one or many images.
  • Combine images: Join images side by side, stacked, or in a grid, as one picture.
  • Palette from image: Pull the main colours out of any image as HEX codes you can copy.
  • Base64 to image: Turn a Base64 string or data URI back into an image you can save.
  • Border and rounded corners: Add a border, padding or rounded corners to images.
  • Split image: Cut an image into a grid of tiles, for carousels and puzzles.
  • SVG to PNG: Turn SVG graphics into sharp PNG images at 1×, 2× or 4× size.
  • Meme maker: Add classic top and bottom meme text to any picture.
  • Social media resizer: Resize images for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and X.
