PDF tools

  • Protect PDF: Add a password to a PDF with strong AES-256 encryption.

  • Crop PDF: Trim the margins of PDF pages by the amounts you choose.
  • Headers and footers: Add text to the top and bottom of every page, with page numbers, totals, dates and the file name.

  • Flatten PDF form: Lock filled-in form fields into the page so they can’t be edited.

  • Compare PDFs: See which lines of text changed between two versions of a PDF.
