PDF tools

Merge, split, organize, compress and convert PDFs.

  • Merge PDF: Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.
  • Split PDF: Break a PDF into separate files by page ranges, single pages, or every few pages.
  • Extract PDF pages: Copy the pages you pick into a new PDF, in the order you list them.
  • Delete PDF pages: Remove unwanted pages from a PDF.
  • Organize PDF: See every page as a thumbnail, then reorder, rotate or delete pages.
  • Rotate PDF: Turn all pages, or only the pages you choose, by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.
  • Reverse page order: Flip the order of pages so the last page comes first.
  • Add page numbers: Stamp page numbers on a PDF, with your choice of position and format.
  • Add watermark: Place a text watermark such as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL across PDF pages.
  • Compress PDF: Make a PDF smaller, losslessly or by converting pages to optimised images.
  • Images to PDF: Turn JPG, PNG and WebP images into a single PDF.
  • PDF to JPG: Save PDF pages as JPG images at the resolution you need.
  • PDF to PNG: Save PDF pages as sharp, lossless PNG images.
  • PDF to text: Pull the text out of a PDF so you can copy, edit or search it.
  • Text to PDF: Turn plain text into a clean, paginated PDF.
  • Edit PDF metadata: Change a PDF’s title, author, subject and keywords.
  • Remove PDF metadata: Strip the author, title, software and XMP details hidden in a PDF.
  • Sign PDF: Draw, type or upload a signature or stamp and place it on your PDF pages.
  • Protect PDF: Add a password to a PDF with strong AES-256 encryption.
  • Unlock PDF: Remove the password from a PDF you’re allowed to open, so other tools can work with it.
  • Crop PDF: Trim the margins of PDF pages by the amounts you choose.
  • Headers and footers: Add text to the top and bottom of every page, with page numbers, totals, dates and the file name.
  • Pages per sheet: Put 2, 4, 6, 8, 9 or 16 pages on each sheet to save paper when printing.
  • Grayscale PDF: Convert a PDF to black and white for cheaper printing.
  • OCR: image or PDF to text: Read the text in photos, screenshots and scanned PDFs, in English and বাংলা.
  • Flatten PDF form: Lock filled-in form fields into the page so they can’t be edited.
  • PDF to WebP: Save PDF pages as small, sharp WebP images.
  • Compare PDFs: See which lines of text changed between two versions of a PDF.
